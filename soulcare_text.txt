2. Moderator Introduction & Communication
SoulCare is a mobile healthcare platform designed to reduce the mental, emotional, and logistical burden faced by adult children caring for elderly parents.
The platform focuses on family coordination, medication adherence, and elder-friendly interaction, addressing gaps left by existing single-user health apps.

3. Before We Start
A. Purpose of the Test
	•	Understand how users interact with SoulCare
	•	Identify usability issues
	•	Improve user experience and accessibility

B. Confidentiality
	•	Your data will remain confidential
	•	The session may be recorded only for research purposes

C. Test Structure & Duration
	•	Total Time: ~30–45 minutes
	•	Includes:
	•	Background questions
	•	Task-based testing
	•	Feedback survey

D. Think Aloud Protocol
Please speak your thoughts while using the app:
	•	What you expect to happen
	•	What feels confusing
	•	What feels easy or helpful

E. Roles & Responsibilities
Moderator
	•	Guides session
	•	Asks questions
	•	Observes behaviour
Participant
	•	Uses app naturally
	•	Shares honest thoughts
	•	Asks questions if confused

4. Background Questions (Interview Section)
	•	How familiar are you with prescriptions and family wellbeing apps?
	•	Do you currently track medicines, habits, or mental wellbeing?
	•	What motivates you to use wellness apps?
	•	What frustrates you about existing wellness apps?

6. Assistance Levels
Level 1  User completes task independently
Level 2  Moderator gives directional hint
Level 3  Moderator explains exact action

7. Post-Test Survey
Ask:
	•	How did you feel using the product?
	•	What did you like the most in terms of visuals and functionality?
	•	Did you feel confused or stuck somewhere while doing tasks?
	•	Would you use this regularly?
	•	Is there anything you want to improve if you were the designer?
